00 The problem

The listing economy runs on trust it can't always prove.

Photos get angled. "Fast wifi" means 10 Mbps in one listing and a gigabit in the next. "Great for remote work" means whatever the host needs it to mean. The amenities guests rely on most — the ones they're actually booking on — are the ones least likely to be verified.

The chair, the lighting, the climate control, the surge protection — every listing claims them, almost none verify them.

Guests who need a stay to actually work end up guessing. Hosts who do invest in real amenities have no way to show it. Both sides lose to the listing willing to oversell. TeoVerified closes that gap.

03 How it works

Two steps. And done.

After you sign up, the path is the same whether you're hands-on or hands-off. The product adapts to you, not the other way around.

i
Step one
Verify your amenities.

For connection performance, we send a test link — open it on your home wifi and the test runs on its own. For workspace and essentials, upload a few photos or just confirm what's already in your listing. We'll clearly label what's been independently verified and what you've reported yourself.

ii
Step two
Place the pill — your credential — on your listing.

We send you an embed snippet (or a badge-image link, if your listing platform doesn't allow code). Place it once. It updates automatically when you re-verify.

Retest, update amenities, or get a new pill anytime — it all happens in your dashboard.
